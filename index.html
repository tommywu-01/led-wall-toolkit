Make a pixel-exact pixel map, a 3D model for your media server, and a full stage layout. Nothing to install, no sign-up, and your files never leave your computer.

▦

Pixel Mapper

Draw the exact pixel map for any cabinet grid and export a 1:1 PNG.

  • Cabinet numbers, signal path and data ports
  • Fit cabinets to a target size in ft, m or in
  • Power, amps and build-time estimates
  • CSV cabinet list and shareable links
Open Pixel Mapper →
◧

OBJ Generator

Turn a flat or U-shaped wall into an OBJ + MTL with clean UVs.

  • Per-cabinet boxes at real scale (mm, m or 0–1)
  • Continuous UVs that line up with your pixel map
  • Live 3D preview with your texture
Open OBJ Generator →
◩

Stage Studio

Lay out several walls, floors and ceilings in 3D and export them all.

  • Snapping, pivots and exact transforms
  • Save and load layouts
  • Optional AI preview with your own key
Open Stage Studio →
